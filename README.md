## 🌟 Introduction

telegram-mcp is a powerful tool for managing multiple Telegram accounts. This application is designed for users who want to streamline their messaging experience and utilize Telegram's features effectively. With telegram-mcp, you can send bulk messages, schedule tasks, and even polish your text with AI tools. It also includes a convenient web dashboard for easy management of various accounts.

## 🌈 Features

- **Multiple Account Management:** Manage several Telegram accounts without logging in and out.
- **Bulk Messaging:** Send messages to multiple contacts at once.
- **AI Tools:** Utilize 123+ AI tools for various tasks.
- **Task Scheduler:** Set up scheduled messages or tasks.
- **Web Dashboard:** Access a user-friendly web interface for account management.
- **Login via QR Code:** Quickly log into your accounts using QR code scanning.
- **Cloud Browser:** Drive a hosted browser you already own for web logins, including your Cloud Phone's Telegram web sign-in. See [docs/CLOUD_BROWSER.md](docs/CLOUD_BROWSER.md).
- **Auto Reply, Knowledge Base and Copilot History:** Answer inbound private chats with your own rules or approved AI drafts, keep operator notes in one encrypted knowledge store, and turn repeated wording into reusable suggestions. Ships switched off. See [docs/AUTO_REPLY_AND_KNOWLEDGE.md](docs/AUTO_REPLY_AND_KNOWLEDGE.md).
- **Inbox:** One live wall of every chat on the accounts you opt in, filed as personal, group, channel or bot, updating as Telegram pushes — no polling, no refresh. Opening a chat marks it read; a reply is only ever text you typed. Ships switched off and stores only your opt-ins and unread counters. See [docs/INBOX.md](docs/INBOX.md).
- **Ops Copilot:** Describe work in your own words from the floating bubble on every page. Its **Ask/Work** switch sits on the left of the composer: Ask reads and drafts, while Work also runs the safe internal writes (creating or pausing an automation) and opens any surface prefilled inside hard bounds, leaving consent unchecked and every operator gate to you. A **Superpowers** row lists all internal commands as one-press chips, attachments accept any file type — PDF, Word, PowerPoint, spreadsheets, OpenDocument, RTF and zips are read as text — and suggestions, prompt enhance and next-step chips are grounded in your encrypted knowledge and chat history. Its preset runbook edits itself to your usage and exports your knowledge base as a ZIP. See [docs/OPS_COPILOT.md](docs/OPS_COPILOT.md).
- **Internal automation:** Create recurring cycles that run with your own account — a scheduled member scrape and a scheduled auto join, each bounded and visible in the Automation tab — while actions that contact other people stay on their own consent-gated surfaces. See [docs/INTERNAL_AUTOMATION.md](docs/INTERNAL_AUTOMATION.md).
- **Free MTProto proxies and re-login:** Browse three public MTProto lists as cards, prove a relay with a real handshake, and save it globally or per account — and restore a logged-out account in place, keeping its ID, usage and proxy, when Telegram reports the same user. See [docs/PROXY_CATALOG_AND_RELOGIN.md](docs/PROXY_CATALOG_AND_RELOGIN.md).
- **Compact record lists:** Job history, chats, contacts, logs and presets render as short collapsed heads with one button for the rest, so a section never turns into a long scroll as records pile up. See [docs/RECORD_LISTS.md](docs/RECORD_LISTS.md).
